Custom Wazuh Detection Rule: Mimikatz Detection (local_rules.xml)

To detect credential theft attempts in real time, I created a custom rule with maximum severity in /var/ossec/etc/rules/local_rules.xml:

```xml
<group name="local,syslog,sshd,">
  <rule id="100002" level="15">
    <if_group>sysmon_event1</if_group>
    <field name="win.eventdata.originalFileName" type="pcre2">(?i)mimikatz\.exe</field>
    <description>Mimikatz Usage Detected</description>
    <mitre>
      <id>T1003</id>
    </mitre>
  </rule>
</group>

Alert Trigger & Rule Validation

After restarting the Wazuh manager, I tested the effectiveness of the new rule by running the Mimikatz utility under a renamed name (youareawesome.exe).

    SIEM Result: Rule 100002 triggered instantly in the Dashboard (Security events), generating a Level 15 severity alert.
    Proof of Detection: Even though the executable name was modified by the attacker, the system correctly identified the threat as Mimikatz Usage Detected, automatically classifying it under the Credential Access tactic (MITRE T1003).
